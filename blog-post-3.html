10 Best Smart Home Devices to Transform Your Space in 2026

January 5, 202611 min readBy 10Best Team

The smart home industry has exploded in recent years. With improvements in AI, Matter compatibility, and energy efficiency, building a smart home has never been easier or more rewarding.

Our Testing Criteria

When evaluating smart home devices, we consider setup ease, app quality, reliability, compatibility, build quality, and value.

1. Amazon Echo Show 10 (Best Smart Display)

The Echo Show 10 remains our top pick for smart displays thanks to its rotating screen, excellent camera, and seamless Alexa integration.

SpecDetails
Display10.1 inch Full HD
Camera13MP with auto-framing
Price$249

2. Google Nest Hub Max

For Google ecosystem users, the Nest Hub Max offers superior smart home controls and excellent Google Assistant integration.

3. Philips Hue Starter Kit

Smart lighting is the gateway to the smart home. Philips Hue remains the king with 16 million colors and seamless platform integration.

4. Ring Video Doorbell Pro 2

Stunning 1536p video, excellent motion detection, and seamless Alexa integration make this a top choice for home security.

5. Ecobee SmartThermostat Premium

Room-sensing technology adjusts temperature based on where you are. Built-in air quality monitor tracks VOCs and humidity.

The biggest trend we see in 2026 is Matter adoption. More devices support it, and the setup experience has become remarkably smooth.

6. Wyze Cam v4 (Best Budget Camera)

2K HDR video, color night vision, person and package detection all for under $40.

7. August Smart Lock Pro + Connect

Keyless entry installed on your existing deadbolt in minutes. Grant temporary access to guests and delivery people.

8. Roborock S8 Pro Ultra

The Swiss Army knife of robot vacuums. Vacuums, mops, empties its own dustbin, and washes its own mop pads.

9. Sonos Era 100

Rich, room-filling sound with multi-room audio support. Perfect for music lovers who want smart convenience.

10. TP-Link Kasa Smart Plugs

The simplest way to smartify any device. Reliable, affordable, and works with Alexa and Google Assistant.

Getting Started

Start small with one area like lighting or security. Choose Matter-compatible devices for maximum future-proofing.
